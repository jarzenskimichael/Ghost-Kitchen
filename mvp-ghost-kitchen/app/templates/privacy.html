MVP GHOST KITCHEN

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Chef MJs Feel Good BBQ LLC, doing business as Merriman Valley Pizza

Business identity

MVP Ghost Kitchen is operated by Chef MJs Feel Good BBQ LLC, doing business as Merriman Valley Pizza ("MVP," "we," or "us").

Information we collect

We collect information submitted through the business application, which may include a contact name, business name, email address, phone number, business details, menu and concept information, licensing details, insurance carrier, policy number, coverage amount, policy expiration date, and uploaded food photos or sample menus. Applicants may also upload a certificate of insurance.

How we use information

We use application information to review and administer applications, communicate about the program, manage individual Monday bookings, operate the website, and maintain program records. Insurance details and certificates are used to evaluate and verify coverage for businesses renting the kitchen. Insurance documents are stored with the application record and are available only to authorized program organizers. We may use contact details to respond to an applicant about their application.

Email communications

We use the email address provided with an application to send application receipts, application decisions, and important scheduling or program updates. We also send internal application notifications to the program organizer. Email is used for operational communication about the Ghost Kitchen program, not unrelated marketing.

Disclosure and security

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers supporting the website and program, or when required to comply with law or protect the rights, safety, and security of MVP and others. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention and choices

We retain application and operational information for as long as reasonably needed to administer the program, maintain business records, resolve issues, and meet legal obligations. You may contact MVP to ask about or request correction of information you submitted.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the program changes. The current version will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact Merriman Valley Pizza through its established business contact channels.
