THE DETAILS

How the program
works.

A straightforward opportunity for food entrepreneurs to operate from the MVP kitchen on Mondays.

One operator. Two consecutive Mondays.

Each rotation gives one approved business exclusive use of the designated ghost kitchen space for two consecutive Monday services. We limit the program to one operator at a time because our kitchen needs to work for everyone.

${{ rental_rate }} per Monday${{ rental_rate * 2 }} total for the two-Monday rotation. Flat rental fee. No percentage of sales.

Who should apply?

Food trucks, caterers, pop-ups, established food businesses, and entrepreneurs developing a new concept are welcome to apply. Every concept is reviewed individually.

What happens after you apply?

  1. Submit your business information, concept, proposed menu, and preferred dates.
  2. MVP reviews your application, menu, equipment needs, and requested dates.
  3. If approved, MVP assigns a two-Monday rotation and confirms the details.
  4. Payment and required documentation must be completed before the first service.

What should operators plan to provide?

Operators should plan to bring their own ingredients, supplies, staff, and any specialty equipment they need. Access to MVP equipment is subject to prior approval. Operators are responsible for following all applicable food-safety requirements and the kitchen's cleaning and operating procedures.

Important to know

  • Applications are requests, not reservations.
  • Only one outside business may operate per rotation.
  • Menus and concepts require MVP approval.
  • Returning businesses must submit a new application.
  • Licensing, insurance, operating hours, and final rental terms will be confirmed before approval is finalized.
